# FSSAI milk-composition test dataset

`ai_datasets/fssai_milk_quality_dataset.csv` is generated by
`generate_fssai_synthetic_milk_dataset.py`. It contains 1,200 deliberately
test records (120 for each FSSAI milk class) for interface, database, and
rule-screen demonstrations.

It is intentionally separate from `milk_training_template.csv` and must not be
treated as laboratory evidence, an expert review, a regulatory certificate, or
production AI training data.

Each row is evaluated independently for:

- the class-specific milk-fat limit;
- the class-specific SNF minimum;
- total sodium at or below 650 mg per 100 g SNF; and
- total urea at or below 700 ppm.

The generator returns only either `PASS` or `HOLD`. Those are test workflow labels;
they are not FSSAI A/B/C quality grades and are not legal compliance decisions.

Reference baseline: FSSAI Food Products Standards and Food Additives
Regulations, 2011, sub-regulation 2.1.2, as reproduced in the dairy compendium
Version XXV (23 September 2022). The Gazette text and later amendments remain
authoritative and must be checked before any real-world use.
